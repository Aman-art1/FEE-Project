Our Story

KarigarCo was born from a simple belief — that the skilled hands of India's artisans deserve a platform as refined as their craft. We connect traditional craftspeople from villages across India directly with customers who value authenticity.

Every product on KarigarCo is handmade, ethically sourced, and comes with the story of the artisan who created it. When you buy here, you support a family, preserve a tradition, and own something truly one of a kind.
